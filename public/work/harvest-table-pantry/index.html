Our mission

Neighbors feeding neighbors

No one in Millbrook should have to choose between groceries and the electric bill.

Harvest Table started in a church basement in 2009 with two shelves of canned goods. Today it is a small grocery-style pantry where families choose their own food.

Volunteers and local donors stock every shelf. Most of the food comes from area farms, grocery stores and community food drives.

What we do

Our programs

Every program is free. If you are not sure which one fits, ask at the front desk.

  • Weekly pantry

    Choose your own groceries on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, like a small grocery store.

  • Weekend backpacks

    Kid-friendly food sent home from two Millbrook elementary schools every Friday.

  • Senior delivery

    Groceries brought to the door for neighbors over 65 who cannot get out.

  • Garden share

    Fresh vegetables from our community garden, June through October.
