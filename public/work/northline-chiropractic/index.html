Your first visit, step by step

No rush and no surprises. You will know what we found and what we suggest before any treatment begins.

  1. One

    An unhurried first visit

    A new patient exam. We talk about what hurts, when it started and what you would like to get back to.

  2. Two

    A clear plan first

    Dr. Ellison explains what he found in plain words and agrees on a plan with you before treatment starts.

  3. Three

    Care that fits your week
