Engines, transmissions and differentials

A JASPER Preferred Installer

For any engine, transmission or differential replacement, we recommend JASPER remanufactured gas and diesel engines, transmissions and drive trains, made in the USA since 1942.

JASPER Preferred Installers attend JASPER Technical Training classes, have toured JASPER’s remanufacturing facility, and are large volume installers of their products. Ask us about replacing the engine, transmission or differential in your car or truck.

Do it right or do it twice

Ray & Roy's has been fixing cars and trucks in Spokane since 1949, and the shop is family owned and operated. We believe in the old phrase "do it right or do it twice", so every repair is done right the first time.

From an oil change to an engine rebuild, on the family car or a company fleet, our ASE-certified technicians are ready for whatever your vehicle needs. We fully explain each repair and give you an estimate or quote before any work begins.
