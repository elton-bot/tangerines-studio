Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and who else is involved. Short, because we collect very little.

Last updated 25 October 2026

What we collect

  • What you send us by email: your name, email address, business name, website and any content you share for your site.
  • Public information from your business's own website, such as your logo, photos, services and contact details, which we use to build your free homepage.

How we use it

We use your email and the details you send to reply to you and to deliver the work: your homepage preview, your site and any care plan edits.

Payments

Card payments are handled by Stripe. Stripe processes your card details; we never see or store them.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies. If that changes, we will update this page first.

Server logs

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, browser type and the pages requested, to run and secure the site.

Homepage previews

Your free homepage preview is published at an unlisted address that search engines are told not to index. You can ask us to take it down at any time.
