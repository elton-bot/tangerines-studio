Terms of service

The plain-English terms for working with Tangerines Studio. If anything here is unclear, email us before you pay.

Last updated 25 October 2026

Who we are

Tangerines Studio designs and builds websites for local service businesses in the United States. It is run by Elton Aclao. Mailing address: 30 N Gould St, STE R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA. Email: elton@tangerinesstudio.com.

What we build

Websites of up to 5 pages for owner-run, single-location service businesses. We don't build online stores, booking systems, member logins or sites of more than 5 pages.

The free homepage

Your new homepage comes first and is free. It is ready within a day and built from your business's own logo, photos and services. You are under no obligation to buy anything. You can ask us to take your homepage preview down at any time.

Prices

  • One-page site: $297, one time.
  • Full site, up to 5 pages: $497, one time. This is a launch price for our first 10 clients.
  • Care plan after launch: $49 a month or $490 a year.

We agree on one flat price with you in writing before any work starts.

Payment

Payments are made by card through Stripe. Stripe processes your card details; we never see them.

Timing

The full site is ready for your review within 3 business days of getting your content. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

Your domain, email and content

Your domain and email stay yours. At launch, only the website records for your domain change, and we send step-by-step instructions for your provider. Your content, business name and logo are yours. If you leave the care plan, you keep your site files.

Care plan

The care plan covers hosting, security and small edits. Send edits by email to support@tangerinesstudio.com and they are done within 2 business days. You can cancel anytime, with no further charges, and keep your site files.

Refunds

The homepage preview is free. Once your finished site has been handed over to you, payments are non-refundable. Care plan payments aren't refunded, but you can cancel the plan anytime and you won't be charged again.

How we work

We work by email, so every answer and price is in writing. We don't do sales calls.

Questions

For a new website, email elton@tangerinesstudio.com. If you are a client, email support@tangerinesstudio.com.
