What we treat

If you are not sure whether we can help, call and ask. We will tell you honestly.

  • Back and neck pain

    Hands-on care and exercise for pain that keeps coming back.

  • Sports injuries

    Sprains, strains and overuse injuries, with a plan to return to play.

  • After surgery

    Rehab after knee, hip and shoulder surgery.

  • Balance and falls

    Strength and balance work to help you feel steady again.

  • Work injuries

    Care and paperwork for injuries that happened on the job.

About us

Small on purpose

Riverbend is a small clinic with two physical therapists. You see the same therapist at every visit.

Visits are one-on-one, so the whole hour is about you.
