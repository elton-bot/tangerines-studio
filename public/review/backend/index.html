The work

Three trades. Three different sites.

These are sample designs for fictional businesses, made to show the standard you can expect. Each one starts from its trade and the questions its customers ask, not from a template.

Juniper Ridge Heating & Air

01 · Heating and airFictional business

Juniper Ridge Heating & Air

A family-owned company in a small town

Design decisions for this trade

  • Evergreen and frost colors that feel local and steady, not corporate.
  • The phone number comes right after the headline, and a call button stays on screen on phones.
  • Services grouped into heating, cooling and air, so people find their problem fast.

Northline Chiropractic

02 · ChiropracticFictional business

Northline Chiropractic

A one-doctor practice

Design decisions for this trade

  • A calm serif and soft colors for a practice where people often arrive in pain.
  • The first visit explained step by step, so a new patient knows what will happen.
  • Hours shown as a week, with the evening days marked, because that is what patients look for.

Harbor Street Auto

03 · Auto repairFictional business

Harbor Street Auto

An independent repair shop

Design decisions for this trade

  • High contrast and condensed type that reads like a shop sign, even in bright sun.
  • The promise of a price before any work, stated up front and again where it matters.
  • Hours like a door sign and a simple street drawing, so drivers find the shop.

Questions

Straight answers.

Something else? Email elton@tangerinesstudio.com and the answer comes back in writing.

Is the homepage really free?

Yes. You see it before anyone mentions money. If it's not for you, there's nothing to cancel.

What do I need to send you?

For the free homepage, just your business name and website address. We build it from your own logo, photos and services.

If you go ahead, send any extra content you want on the full site, such as more photos or details about your services.

How long does it take?

Your free homepage is ready within a day. The full site is ready for your review within 3 business days of getting your content.

Do I keep my domain and email?

Yes. Your domain stays in your name and your email keeps working. Only the website records change, and we send step-by-step instructions for your provider.

Who owns the website?

Your content, name and logo are yours. If you ever leave the care plan, you keep your site files.

Can we talk on the phone?

We work by email, so every answer and price is in writing.

What happens after launch?

The care plan covers hosting, security and small edits. Email support@tangerinesstudio.com with what you'd like changed, and it's done within 2 business days.

Do you build online stores or booking systems?

No. We build websites of up to five pages for local service businesses. Online stores, booking systems, member logins and larger sites are outside what we do.

What's your refund policy?

Your homepage preview is free, so you only pay once you've seen it and want to go ahead. Once your finished site has been handed over to you, payments are non-refundable. Care plan payments aren't refunded either, but you can cancel anytime and you won't be charged again.

How do I get help?

Clients can email support@tangerinesstudio.com anytime. For a new website, email elton@tangerinesstudio.com.
